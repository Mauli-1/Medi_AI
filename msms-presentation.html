Project Overview

Purpose & Goals

This system automates end-to-end pharmacy operations — from medicine procurement and batch-wise stock tracking to GST-compliant billing, prescription management, ABHA integration, and AI-driven demand forecasting. It is designed for Indian retail and wholesale pharmacies operating under Schedule H/H1 compliance requirements.

Key Business Goals
Goal Measurable Outcome
Reduce manual data entry50% reduction in staff data-entry time
Speed up counter billingInvoice generated in under 1 second
Prevent expiry lossesReal-time alerts 30/15/7 days before expiry
GST complianceAuto-calculated taxes; GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B ready
Inventory turnoverNon-moving and dead stock identified automatically

Full system control — user management, configurations, all modules with CRUD and reports.

Business setup, financial overview, subscription management, all business and financial data.
Pharmacist
Prescription verification, dispensing, counseling. Billing requires approval for Schedule H drugs.
Cashier
Counter billing, payment collection, returns, and customer lookup.
Store Manager
Inventory oversight, staff coordination, daily operations, sales reports.
Inventory Manager
Stock entry, adjustments, audits, inter-branch transfers, and stock reports.
Accountant
Financial entries, GST filing, ledgers, P&L, and accounting reports.
Auditor
Read-only access across all modules for compliance review — no data modifications.
Security: 2FA via OTP (SMS/Email), configurable session timeout (default 30 min), complete activity logs for every login and data change.

AI & Smart Automation

Module 28 · 14 Features
AI Prescription Reader
OCR + Named Entity Recognition to extract medicine names, dosage, and duration from handwritten prescriptions.
AI Purchase Bill OCR
Extracts medicine name, batch, expiry, rate, and GST from supplier invoices (photo or PDF).
AI Medicine Recognition
Identifies a medicine from a photo of the packaging or blister strip.
AI Sales Forecasting
Predicts daily and weekly sales volumes by medicine category.
AI Demand Forecasting
Seasonal and regional demand prediction to pre-empt stockouts.
AI Inventory Forecasting
Calculates optimal stock levels to minimise holding cost and prevent stockouts.
AI Expiry Prediction
Estimates probability that a batch will expire before it is sold, so action can be taken early.
AI Dead Stock Prediction
Flags medicines likely to become dead stock before 180-day threshold is reached.
AI Reorder Prediction
Suggests optimal reorder quantity and timing per medicine.
AI Generic Substitute
Recommends bioequivalent generics at lower cost when a brand is out of stock or expensive.
AI Chatbot
Answers FAQs, guides billing flow, and responds to stock queries in natural language.
AI Voice Assistant
Voice-controlled billing, medicine search, and purchase entry in English, Hindi, and Marathi.
AI Supplier Analysis
Compares suppliers on price, delivery reliability, and quality history.
AI Duplicate Invoice Detection
Prevents the same purchase invoice from being entered twice, even with OCR variation.
